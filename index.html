About New Prime

A crew built on trust.

Our company

One reliable crew for every part of your property.

For 25 years, from 2001 to present, New Prime General Construction Corp. has helped New York property owners protect, repair and improve the places they depend on.

2001Established in New York

Established in 2001, our fully insured company has worked on a wide variety of residential and commercial projects—from roofing, waterproofing and masonry to complete interior and exterior renovations.

Our experience includes working as a New York City HPD contractor, giving our team practical familiarity with the standards, coordination and care required for city-related housing work.

New Prime is licensed to work in Yonkers and Westchester County and proudly serves customers throughout all five boroughs of New York City: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island.

We believe good construction begins with clear communication. We explain the work, respect the property and stay focused on clean, durable results that clients can trust.

Our record matters: New Prime has no history of unfinished or fraudulent work. We stand behind our commitments and approach every project with honesty, accountability and respect for our customers.

Common questions

Are estimates free?

Yes. Confirm whether specialized written reports or diagnostic services ever carry a fee.

Do you handle permits?

Permit requirements and responsibility should be identified in writing for each project.

Can customers send photographs?

Yes. The enhanced request form allows customers to preview selected project images before submitting.

When does work begin?
